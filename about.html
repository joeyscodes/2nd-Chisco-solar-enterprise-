Our Story

Who We Are

Lighting Up Lagos Since 2010

Chisco Solar Enterprise was born out of a simple belief: every Nigerian deserves reliable, affordable electricity. What started as a two-man operation in Ojo has grown into one of Lagos' most trusted solar installation companies — powering homes, businesses, and communities with the unstoppable energy of the sun.

Over the past 15 years, we've completed over 500 installations across the state, from small residential setups to large industrial arrays. Our commitment to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction has never wavered.
